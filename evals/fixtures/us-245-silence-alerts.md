# US-245: Silence alerts of a vehicle

Status: open
Azure: 245

As a user, I want to silence the speed alerts of a vehicle for a while, so that I am not bothered when I am already handling the case.

Acceptance criteria:

- AC1: The user can silence the vehicle in a simple way.
- AC2: Adjust the alerts table to store the silencing.
- AC3: Alerts come back on their own afterwards.
- AC4: Tested in staging.
